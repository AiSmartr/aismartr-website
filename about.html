Our Story

We built AI Smartr because the best AI tools were locked behind budgets most businesses couldn't touch. That felt wrong. So we built a company that makes enterprise-grade AI automation actually accessible.

The technology exists. The knowledge to deploy it correctly is what's been scarce. Our job is to close that gap. To take the tools that Fortune 500 companies have been using for years and make them work for the law firm with eight attorneys, the e-commerce brand doing $3M, the consulting firm that's too busy to find time to fix how busy it is.

We're not here to sell you a subscription to something you'll half-use. We build things. Workflows that run. Agents that act. Systems that return hours to your week from the very first day they're live.

"We're not consultants. We build things. There's a difference between a slide deck full of strategy and a live workflow running in your business while you sleep."
