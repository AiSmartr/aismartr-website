Common Questions

FAQ.

How long does implementation take?

Most single workflows are live within one to two weeks. More complex builds with multiple integrations or custom AI agents typically take three to six weeks. We scope everything upfront so there are no surprises on timeline.

Do I need technical expertise?

No. You need to understand your business processes. We handle everything technical. We'll ask you a lot of questions about how things work and what you need, but none of those questions require you to know anything about code or infrastructure.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Security is a design requirement for everything we build, not an add-on. Every integration uses scoped, least-privilege access. Sensitive data can be processed on-premise or through enterprise API agreements with appropriate data handling terms. We'll walk you through the security model for your specific build.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's exactly what the consultation is for. Most clients come in with a vague sense of "we're spending too much time on X" and leave with a clear picture of what to automate first and what the return would look like. You don't need to have it figured out before we talk.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We offer ongoing monitoring, optimization, and expansion packages for clients who want a long-term partner. Most clients find that once the first automation is running, they immediately want to build the second. We can support that roadmap on an ongoing basis.
