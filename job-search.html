Questions

Straight answers.

Do you submit applications for me?
No — and that's intentional. We find the jobs and tailor your CV for each one. You submit. This keeps you in control, your accounts safe, and the applications genuinely yours. With pre-built CVs, each submission takes about 2 minutes.
What if the jobs don't match what I'm looking for?
That's rare, but if your results don't include at least 10 genuinely relevant roles, we'll rerun the scan against adjusted criteria at no charge. We want you to have a usable pipeline, not a list of noise.
What roles and industries do you cover?
We focus on tech, AI, SaaS, fintech, and professional services. We cover roles from IC to Director level — engineering, product, sales engineering, operations, and AI/ML. If you're in a niche industry, tell us upfront and we'll confirm coverage before you pay.
How do you tailor the CVs?
The AI reads your resume and the job description side by side — it identifies which of your experiences are most relevant, adjusts your summary, reorders bullets, and injects the right keywords for ATS. It's not a template swap. Each CV is reasoning about your fit for that specific role.
Is my resume data private?
Yes. We use your resume only to run your evaluation. We don't store, share, or use it for anything else. Your data is processed and then deleted within 7 days of delivery.
