For Law Firms

Rules Come First.

New York lawyers have more guidance to read than most. The New York State Bar Association's AI task force published its recommendations in April 2024, and the ABA's Formal Opinion 512 sets out how professional responsibility duties apply to generative AI. We built a checklist that turns those duties into controls for AI agents.

Read the Opinion 512 Checklist
  • Scoped access. Each agent sees only the matters and systems it needs.
  • Approval gates. Attorneys approve anything that carries legal judgment.
  • A full log. You can see what every agent did, and who approved it.
  • No legal advice from agents. Attorneys make the calls.
