What We Build

Admin That
Never Bills.

Attorneys lose a large share of their time to work that never shows up on an invoice. That's the layer agents are good at.

Client intake

Agents answer inquiries at any hour, ask your intake questions, and collect the documents a matter needs.

Document assembly

Engagement letters, forms, and routine documents are drafted from your templates for attorney review.

First-pass document review

Agents flag clauses, missing items, and differences against your standards so attorneys start with a map.

Deadline tracking

Dates and tasks are tracked and reminders go out on schedule, so nothing depends on memory.

Time entry and billing

Draft time entries and clear billing narratives come from calendars and documents, ready to confirm.

Client updates

Status updates are drafted from the matter file for the responsible attorney to review and send.

Example

A new inquiry at 7:15 p.m.

  1. A prospective client submits an inquiry after the office closes.
  2. The agent asks your standard intake questions and collects the documents they have.
  3. It prepares the party information for your conflict-check process and flags the matter for staff.
  4. It drafts the engagement letter from your template and queues it for attorney approval.
  5. It offers consultation times from the attorney's calendar. The prospect books one.

Control

Attorneys Make The Calls.

  • Judgment stays human. Agents prepare and organize. Attorneys review everything that carries legal judgment before it reaches a client.
  • No legal advice from agents. Agents handle intake, drafting support, and admin. They don't advise clients.
  • Confidentiality by design. We scope each agent's access to the matters and systems it needs and log every action.
  • An audit trail. You can see what was drafted, what was sent, and who approved it.

Questions

Common
Questions.

Can AI make mistakes on legal documents?

Yes, which is why attorney review is built into every workflow that touches legal judgment. Agents produce drafts and flags. Your attorneys approve.

How do you protect client confidentiality?

Agents get scoped, least-privilege access, approval gates on sensitive actions, and a full log. We walk through the setup with you before anything goes live.

Does it connect to our practice management software?

We connect to the tools you already use, and we confirm the options for your systems on the first call.

Is this only for large firms?

No. Solo attorneys and small firms often feel the admin load most. We scope the build to your size.
