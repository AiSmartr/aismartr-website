Workflow Automation

We map your process, find the waste, and build pipelines that handle the repetitive work. Lead follow-up, onboarding, invoicing, approvals, internal handoffs. If it follows a pattern, it can probably run without you.

Most businesses are surprised how much of what their team does every day is actually just rule-following. We document that logic, build it into automated pipelines, and hand you back the hours.

You get consistency along with the speed. Every lead gets followed up.

Every client gets onboarded correctly. Every invoice goes out on time.

  • Lead follow-up and nurture sequences
  • Client onboarding and offboarding
  • Invoice generation and payment reminders
  • Internal approval routing
  • Weekly and monthly reporting pipelines
  • Multi-step cross-system workflows
02

AI Agents

We build agents for real estate, finance, legal, and professional services firms. They handle customer support, sales and marketing, finance and back office, and IT operations. They research, reach out, book meetings, process invoices, and run workflows without anyone watching. Around the clock.

A chatbot answers a question. An AI agent reads your email, pulls the relevant context from your CRM, drafts a response, sends it, logs the interaction, and schedules the next follow-up. Before you've finished your coffee.

That's what we build. Agents that handle multi-step work autonomously, escalate when something genuinely needs a human, and get smarter about your business over time.

  • Sales: lead qualification, outreach, and pipeline follow-up
  • Marketing: content drafts, campaign reporting, and social scheduling
  • Finance: invoice processing, reconciliation, and payment reminders
  • HR: resume screening, onboarding, and employee requests
  • Customer support: ticket triage, order lookups, and replies
  • Operations: reporting, scheduling, and vendor follow-up
