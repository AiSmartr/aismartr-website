Questions

Common
Questions.

What do you build?

AI agents for customer support, sales and marketing, finance and back office, and IT operations, plus the workflows, integrations, and custom apps that connect them to the tools you already use.

Who do you build for?

Real estate, finance, legal, and professional services firms, from independent practices to mid-market companies.

How long does a first build take?

Most first builds go live in a few weeks. We scope the work up front, so you know the plan and the timeline before anything gets built.

What does it cost?

Fixed-price builds scoped up front, plus flat monthly retainers to keep everything running and growing. You get a clear price on the free call before you commit to anything.

Do I need technical expertise?

No. You need to understand your business processes. We handle the technical work and explain what the technology does in plain English.

Is my data secure?

Security is part of the design. Each agent gets scoped, least-privilege access, approval gates on sensitive actions, and a log of everything it does.
